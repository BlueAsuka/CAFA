EXAMPLE: continuous_blend
QUESTION:
Blend exactly 10 tonnes of two divisible materials. A costs 4 per tonne and contains 20% protein; B costs 6 and contains 50% protein. The blend must contain at least 32% protein. Minimize total cost.
DATA:
{}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='continuous_blend')
a = m.addVar(lb=0, ub=10, name='tonnes_A', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS)
b = m.addVar(lb=0, ub=10, name='tonnes_B', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS)
m.setObjective(4 * a + 6 * b, GRB.MINIMIZE)
m.addConstr(a + b == 10, name='total_tonnes')
m.addConstr(0.2 * a + 0.5 * b >= 0.32 * 10, name='protein_tonnes')

EXAMPLE: integer_production
QUESTION:
Make whole units of products A and B. A requires 2 labor hours, B requires 3. There are 9 hours available. Profits are 3 and 5 per unit respectively. Maximize profit using the supplied parameters.
DATA:
{"hours": 9, "labor": [2, 3], "profit": [3, 5]}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='integer_production')
products = range(len(data['profit']))
x = {i: m.addVar(lb=0, name=f'units_{i}', vtype=GRB.INTEGER) for i in products}
m.setObjective(gp.quicksum((data['profit'][i] * x[i] for i in products)), GRB.MAXIMIZE)
m.addConstr(gp.quicksum((data['labor'][i] * x[i] for i in products)) <= data['hours'], name='labor_hours')

EXAMPLE: transportation_table
QUESTION:
Ship divisible tonnes from depots A and B. North needs exactly 4 tonnes and South exactly 3. All routes are allowed. Minimize transportation cost; unused capacity is allowed.
| Depot | Capacity (tonnes) | Cost/tonne to North | Cost/tonne to South |
| A     | 5                 | 1                   | 4                   |
| B     | 4                 | 3                   | 1                   |
DATA:
{}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='transportation_table')
capacity = {'A': 5, 'B': 4}
demand = {'North': 4, 'South': 3}
cost = {('A', 'North'): 1, ('A', 'South'): 4, ('B', 'North'): 3, ('B', 'South'): 1}
x = {(i, j): m.addVar(lb=0, name=f'ship_{i}_{j}', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS) for i in capacity for j in demand}
m.setObjective(gp.quicksum((cost[i, j] * x[i, j] for i in capacity for j in demand)), GRB.MINIMIZE)
for j in demand:
    m.addConstr(gp.quicksum((x[i, j] for i in capacity)) == demand[j], name=f'demand_{j}')
for i in capacity:
    m.addConstr(gp.quicksum((x[i, j] for j in demand)) <= capacity[i], name=f'capacity_{i}')

EXAMPLE: facility_table_milp
QUESTION:
Choose which warehouses to open and ship divisible tonnes. North needs exactly 6 tonnes and South exactly 5. An unopened warehouse cannot ship. An opened warehouse may leave capacity unused. All routes are allowed. Minimize opening plus shipping cost.
| Warehouse | Capacity (tonnes) | Fixed opening cost | Cost/tonne to North | Cost/tonne to South |
| A         | 12                | 10                 | 1                   | 4                   |
| B         | 12                | 12                 | 3                   | 1                   |
DATA:
{}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='facility_table_milp')
capacity = {'A': 12, 'B': 12}
fixed_cost = {'A': 10, 'B': 12}
demand = {'North': 6, 'South': 5}
cost = {('A', 'North'): 1, ('A', 'South'): 4, ('B', 'North'): 3, ('B', 'South'): 1}
y = {i: m.addVar(name=f'open_{i}', vtype=GRB.BINARY) for i in capacity}
x = {(i, j): m.addVar(lb=0, name=f'ship_{i}_{j}', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS) for i in capacity for j in demand}
m.setObjective(gp.quicksum((fixed_cost[i] * y[i] for i in capacity)) + gp.quicksum((cost[i, j] * x[i, j] for i in capacity for j in demand)), GRB.MINIMIZE)
for j in demand:
    m.addConstr(gp.quicksum((x[i, j] for i in capacity)) == demand[j], name=f'demand_{j}')
for i in capacity:
    m.addConstr(gp.quicksum((x[i, j] for j in demand)) <= capacity[i] * y[i], name=f'open_capacity_{i}')

EXAMPLE: inventory_balance
QUESTION:
Produce divisible units over two periods. Demand is 3 then 5 units; capacity is 6 units per period. Unit production cost is 1 then 4. Initial stock is zero, no shortages are allowed, and stock after period 2 must be zero. Holding stock from period 1 to period 2 costs 1 per unit. Minimize total cost.
DATA:
{}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='inventory_balance')
demand = [3, 5]
cost = [1, 4]
production = {t: m.addVar(lb=0, ub=6, name=f'production_{t}', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS) for t in range(2)}
stock = {t: m.addVar(lb=0, name=f'stock_{t}', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS) for t in range(2)}
for t in range(2):
    previous = 0 if t == 0 else stock[t - 1]
    m.addConstr(previous + production[t] == demand[t] + stock[t], name=f'balance_{t}')
m.addConstr(stock[1] == 0, name='final_stock')
m.setObjective(gp.quicksum((cost[t] * production[t] for t in range(2))) + stock[0], GRB.MINIMIZE)

EXAMPLE: machine_scheduling
QUESTION:
Two jobs of duration 3 and 2 must run without interruption on one machine. The first job is released at time 0 and the second at time 1. No overlap is allowed. Both jobs must finish by time 8. Choose their order and minimize the completion time of the last job.
DATA:
{}
CODE:
m = gp.Model(name='machine_scheduling')
start_a = m.addVar(lb=0, ub=5, name='start_a', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS)
start_b = m.addVar(lb=1, ub=6, name='start_b', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS)
a_first = m.addVar(name='a_first', vtype=GRB.BINARY)
finish = m.addVar(lb=0, ub=8, name='finish', vtype=GRB.CONTINUOUS)
m.addConstr(start_a + 3 <= start_b + 8 * (1 - a_first), name='a_before_b')
m.addConstr(start_b + 2 <= start_a + 8 * a_first, name='b_before_a')
m.addConstr(finish >= start_a + 3, name='finish_a')
m.addConstr(finish >= start_b + 2, name='finish_b')
m.setObjective(finish, GRB.MINIMIZE)
